Contextual learning

Visuals in the right place

Use a visual to explain the mathematical idea—not as decoration.

↗
Definition → Concept diagram

Show the objects, relationships, mapping or geometric meaning directly beside the definition.

∴
Theorem → Explanation & example

Place an intuitive visual and a worked verification example near the theorem statement.

▤
Proof → Step-by-step structure

Keep the proof complete, with important logical steps and supporting illustrations where useful.

∿
Method → Flowchart / graph

Use iterations, plots, tables or animations for algorithms and changing quantities.

The source subject files are preserved without rewriting their course material. Existing interactive visualizations remain in those files. This homepage does not claim that a new animation has been inserted beside every theorem; that requires carefully editing and checking each individual lesson.
